# Juegos del Tiempo

A small website with games for practising **telling the time, days of the week and months in Spanish**. It was made for MYP Spanish at CIS Moss.

Students open the link in a browser. They don't need an account or to install anything, and it works on laptops and phones.

## The games

| Game | What students practise |
|---|---|
| **El salto** | They jump onto the platform with the correct answer, with 5 seconds per level. One wrong jump and the game ends. Questions mix times, days and months. |
| **¿Qué hora es?** | Read an analog clock and choose the correct phrase, including *¿A qué hora…?* questions. |
| **Pon el reloj** | Read a phrase and move the clock hands to match it. |
| **Escribe la hora** | Type the time in Spanish (challenge level). |
| **Los días** | Translate days (English and Norwegian) and work out *antes / después / ayer / mañana*. |
| **Los meses** | Month numbers, translations, *antes / después* and the months of festivals. |
| **La carrera** | Put the days and months in order against the clock. |

## Points

- Correct answers earn 10–20 points, and three in a row add a +5 bonus. Wrong answers earn 0 points.
- In **El salto**, every level climbed is worth 10 points.
- At the end of the lesson, students click **Finish practice** to see a summary with their name, the date, their total and a score for each game. They can show it to the teacher or take a screenshot.
- Points are saved only in that student's browser. On shared computers, students click **Reset my points** at the start of the lesson.

## Hosting and updating

The whole site is a single file, `index.html`, published with GitHub Pages (**Settings → Pages → Branch: main / root**).

To update it, upload a new `index.html` and replace the old one. The link stays the same, and changes appear after a minute or two.
